# User Guide: Administrator Role Management

## Overview

This guide outlines the functionality of managing roles within the User Management System, particularly focusing on Administrator roles. Users with appropriate permissions can modify the associated permissions of an Administrator role, allowing for dynamic adjustment of access levels as needed.

---

## Modifying Administrator Role Permissions

### Accessing Role Management

1. **Login**: Ensure you are logged in with an account that has permission to modify roles.
2. **Navigate to Role Management**: From the main dashboard, go to the "Settings" area and select "Role Management."

### Steps to Modify Administrator Role

1. **Select Role**: In the Role Management interface, choose "Administrator" from the list of existing roles.
2. **Edit Permissions**: Click on "Edit Permissions" to modify the permissions associated with the Administrator role.
   - A list of current permissions will be displayed.
   - Check or uncheck permissions as necessary to grant or revoke access to specific features or areas of the system.

3. **Save Changes**: Once adjustments are made, click the "Save" button to apply changes.
   - Changes are reflected in the system immediately and will impact all users with the Administrator role.

### Considerations

- **Validation**: Ensure that changes comply with organizational policies and do not inadvertently restrict critical functionalities.
- **Auditing**: Each modification is logged, and changes can be reviewed by accessing the "Audit Trail" feature within the Role Management area.

### Reverting Permission Changes

1. **Revisit Permissions**: Follow the same steps to access the Administrator role.
2. **Modify as Required**: Undo any previous changes by adjusting the permissions back to their original state.
3. **Confirm and Save**: Confirm the changes and save to update the system settings.
